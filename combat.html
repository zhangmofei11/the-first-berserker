Combat Guide

Master the brutal combat system.

Basic Combat

⚔️ Light Attacks

Fast attacks that build combo meter quickly.

  • • Quick succession attacks
  • • Good for building rage
  • • Low damage per hit

💥 Heavy Attacks

Slow but powerful strikes that stagger enemies.

  • • High damage output
  • • Can interrupt enemy attacks
  • • Builds rage faster

🔥 Enraged State

Unlocked at 30% rage. Increased damage and attack speed.

  • • +20% damage boost
  • • +15% attack speed
  • • Red aura effect

⚡ Berserk Mode

Unlocked at 70% rage. Devastating attacks with unique moves.

  • • +50% damage boost
  • • Special attack abilities
  • • Health regeneration

Combo System

  • • Light x3 → Heavy - Standard combo starter
  • • Heavy → Light x2 → Heavy - Air launch combo
  • • Heavy x3 - Ground slam finisher
  • • Rage activation - Cancel any combo into rage ability

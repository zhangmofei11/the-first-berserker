Tips & Tricks

Essential advice for dominating combat.

Combat Tips

🔥 Manage Your Rage

Don't waste rage on weak enemies. Save it for tough fights and bosses.

⚔️ Master Parrying

Perfect parries deal massive damage and stun enemies. Practice timing!

🎯 Use Environment

Explosive barrels and environmental hazards deal extra damage.

💨 Keep Moving

Standing still gets you hit. Circle enemies and stay mobile.

Rage Tips

  • • Heavy attacks build rage faster - Use them against groups
  • • Get hit to build rage - Taking damage fuels your fury
  • • Berserk mode regenerates health - Use it when low HP
  • • Rage abilities don't cost rage - Only activation has cost

Exploration Tips

🔍 Search Secret Areas

Breakable walls and hidden passages often contain rare weapons.

📦 Complete Side Quests

They provide upgrade materials and unlock powerful abilities.

🏪 Visit Shops Regularly

New weapons and materials appear as you progress.

🔄 Upgrade Often

Don't save materials - regular upgrades make game much easier.

General Tips

Save Often

Multiple save slots prevent progress loss

Study Enemies

Watch attack patterns before engaging

Upgrade Weapons

A upgraded rare beats a weak legendary
